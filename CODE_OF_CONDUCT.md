# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies across all repositories under the [`CitrateNetwork`](https://github.com/CitrateNetwork) organization, all events the project sponsors or attends, and all communication channels (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, the Discord server, email).

## Standards

We expect contributors to:

- **Be respectful.** Critique code, not people. Assume good faith.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcoming to all skill levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- **Be specific.** "Could you explain X?" beats "this is wrong."
- **Be honest.** When you don't know, say so. When you're wrong, say so.
- **Be patient.** People work on different schedules and time zones.

We will not tolerate:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Doxing or sharing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or community spaces.
- Sexual or violent content.
- Soliciting for unrelated commercial purposes.

## Reporting

Conduct concerns: **conduct@citrate.network**. We acknowledge within 5 business days.

Security concerns: see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), use security@citrate.network.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will:

1. **Warn** — for first-offense, low-severity issues. Specific, written, with what changes.
2. **Temp-ban** — for repeated or moderate-severity violations. 7-30 days from project spaces.
3. **Ban** — for severe or sustained violations. Permanent removal from project spaces.

Maintainer enforcement decisions are reviewable by the federation lead. Disagreements about enforcement go to conduct@citrate.network with a `[REVIEW]` subject tag.
