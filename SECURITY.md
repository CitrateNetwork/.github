# Security Policy

## Reporting
Submit vulnerabilities to [security@citrate.ai](mailto:security@citrate.ai).

## Encryption
Public PGP key available at [security@citrate.ai](https://citrate.ai/.well-known/security.txt).

## Rate Limits
- **Per Host**: 5 requests/second, 10,000 requests/day

## Legal
All reports are covered under our [Safe Harbor Policy](https://citrate.ai/legal/safe-harbor).

## Known Issues
Publication of known issues will begin at launch with ID: `CITRATE-KNOWN-001`
